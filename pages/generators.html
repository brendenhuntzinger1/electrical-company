The install

What happens, start to finish.

Most installs take one or two days on site. Permits and gas meter upgrades set the overall timeline, so we start those right away.

  1. Assessment at your homeWe do a load calculation, pick the spot, and check your gas meter and panel.
  2. Written, flat quoteOne price, with financing options if you want them.
  3. Permits and gasWe pull the permits and ask the gas company for a meter upgrade if needed. Propane homes get a tank set up.
  4. Install dayPad, generator, automatic transfer switch, wiring and gas line.
  5. InspectionThe county inspects the electrical and gas work.
  6. Startup and walkthroughWe cut the power to show you the switchover, set the weekly self-test and connect the monitoring app.

Questions

Generator FAQ

Natural gas or propane?

Natural gas if your street has it. It never needs refilling. Without gas, we set up a propane tank with a local supplier.

What's the difference from a portable generator?

A standby generator is permanent and starts itself. No cords, no gas cans, no going outside in a storm. If budget is tight, we also install inlet boxes and interlocks so you can safely plug in a portable.

How often does it need service?

Once a year at minimum: oil, filters, battery and a load test. Club membership covers it, adds remote monitoring, and puts you first in line before storms.

How long does the whole process take?

The install itself is one or two days. From signed quote to running generator usually takes a few weeks, depending on permits and the gas company. Book early in the year to be ready by June.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Ask for monthly options when we send your quote.
