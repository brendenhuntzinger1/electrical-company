Questions

Homeowner FAQ

Is it an emergency?

Call right away if you smell burning, see sparks or scorch marks, have a warm or buzzing panel, or lose power to part of the house. If you smell burning, turn off the main breaker if it's safe to do so.

Do you charge for estimates?

Estimates for planned work are free. Troubleshooting a problem has a flat diagnostic fee that we tell you before we come out.

Do you pull permits?

Yes, on every job that needs one. It protects you when you sell the house or file an insurance claim.
