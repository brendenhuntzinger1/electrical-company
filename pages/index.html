Questions

Good to know.

Something else on your mind? Call or text and a real person answers.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We're a Florida Certified Electrical Contractor (license {LICENSE}) with liability and workers' compensation insurance. You can verify our license on the Florida DBPR website.

Do you charge for estimates?

Estimates for planned work are free. Troubleshooting a problem has a flat diagnostic fee that we tell you before we come out.

Do you pull permits?

Yes, on every job that needs one. It protects you when you sell your home or file an insurance claim.

Is it an emergency?

Call right away if you smell burning, see sparks or scorch marks, have a warm or buzzing panel, or lose power to part of the house. If it's safe to do so, switch off the main breaker.

What areas do you serve?

Hillsborough, Pinellas and Pasco counties, including Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel and Lutz. Outside that? Call anyway.
