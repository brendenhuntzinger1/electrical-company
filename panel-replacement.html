Questions

Panel FAQ

Will my insurer accept the new panel?

We give you the permit, inspection result, photos and invoice, which is what insurers usually ask for. Check with your insurer for anything else they need.

Should I upgrade to 200 amps?

If you're adding a generator, EV charger, pool equipment or more A/C, usually yes. We check your load and tell you straight.

How long will my power be off?

Usually part of one day. We tell you the window ahead of time.
